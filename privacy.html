ScriptFloww · Privacy

Nothing collected. Nothing sent.

ScriptFloww does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

Everything you write stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no server calls except loading Manrope font stylesheets from Google Fonts (a static CSS request; no cookies or identifiers are exchanged).

Writing sessions are held only in browser memory and clear when the timer resets or the app closes. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
